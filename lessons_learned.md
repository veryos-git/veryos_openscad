## Rules

- A part that hangs behind a box tips it: the RGBW puck in its holder, 25.9 behind the back wall of a box 92.5 × 50.6 × 111.6, tipped it backwards (print, 2026-11-25). Carry the side walls on behind the box as feet, past the hanging part's end; where the box is printed on its top, their upper edge climbs at 45 degrees and needs no support (paper_light_box V3: feet 30 × 2, 29 mm of overhang wall sliced; not printed).
- A small light close behind sheets of paper shows as a bright dot: the puck's lens behind a Ø36 window 15 from the last sheet. With more room it spreads, about 30 by the user's trial (paper_light_box V2 print, 2026-11-25; V3 has 30, not printed).
- Paper cannot be pushed, so a sheet has to fall down its grooves: play p beside a sheet h high is used up by a tilt of p / h, 0.5 on 111 by 0.26 degrees. Give paper its room beside it and take it back in the grooves' depth (paper_light_box V2 print, 2026-11-25: 0.25 per side jammed; reasoned, where it stuck not reported).
- A slicer project of a first print names the printer and the settings: read it before choosing what to slice the next version with (paper_light_box: `v1test.3mf` was a Bambu Lab A2L project, the checks before it had used another printer's settings, 2026-11-25).
- A template to trace with a pencil is a positive shape, the size of the piece less the pencil's line: a pencil hardly follows the inside of a 7.5 wide window in a 3 thick plate. A window is for a knife (OMM XY table cutting ruler: v3.0.0's windows tried by the user, v3.0.4's bars not yet; 2026-11-25).
- A thread stands upright in the print. Where a part's own position would lay it down, as in the back wall of a box printed on its top, give the thread a plate of its own that slides in like the box's other loose parts and prints flat: paper_light_box V2 takes the puck holder's top part that way, V1's untested pocket is gone (2026-11-25; sliced, not printed).
- An OpenSCAD variable used in an assignment above its own is undef there, with a warning only: `logo_h = ... logo_r / logo_w` above the logo's block silently dropped the logo. Functions and modules may use it; put such a value below what it needs (paper_light_box V2, 2026-11-25).
- A box that takes loose parts from above prints upside down: the top wall with its slits lies on the bed, the grooves stand upright, the bottom stays open, and stops under the grooves bridge no more than a slit's width. As it stands, its top wall would be a bridge as wide as the box; on its back, every slit a bridge as long as a sheet (paper_light_box V1, 2026-11-25; the box for six sheets sliced 123 g, 5 h 41 min, 0.4 m of bridges, no support; not printed).
- A slit cut down to a stop leaves the stop as a bar across the whole box unless the room under it is cut as well: paper_light_box V1's board slit kept a 3 × 0.6 bar 115 long, a bridge in the print, found only by comparing two builds' vertices. Look at a part from below too (2026-11-25; mesh).
- A tower joined to a block leaves zero-area triangles under Manifold whether it overlaps the block by 0.01 or starts on its face, and so does a cutter whose face lies in a face the block already has. One block, and all that is not the tower cut away in one extruded 2D shape, is clean (paper_light_box V1 puck pocket, 2026-11-25; mesh).
- OpenSCAD 2021.01's STL of a big part is 2 ppm small in volume, 0.2 mm³ of 97 cm³, against Manifold's and the value reckoned by hand: its outer faces are 0.3 ppm short. Compare two builds' volumes relative to the part's size, and their faces plane by plane (paper_light_box V1, 2026-11-25; mesh).
- `linear_extrude(t) import("sheet.svg")` fills a path's sub-paths by the even-odd rule and takes width and height in mm with a viewBox, in OpenSCAD 2021.01 and 2026.09.16: sheets with holes and islands come out closed, their volume the area times t (paper_light_box V1 webapp, 2026-11-25; mesh).
