# Parametric Screw-Top Container V2 — 7 Surface Styles

## Description

A customizable, 3D-printable cylindrical container with a screw-on lid and a choice of seven exterior styles: smooth, fine vertical ribs, broad rounded flutes, spiral ribs, diamond knurl, wavy ribs, and chevron ribs.

V2 adds configurable surface patterns to the original screw-top container. Choose a preset in OpenSCAD, then adjust the diameter, height, wall thickness, lid height, thread pitch, and fit clearances. Pattern depth and spacing are adjustable, and the texture can be enabled independently on the body and lid. The relief adds material outside the nominal diameter while leaving the cavity, neck, and screw fit unchanged.

Use it to organize small hardware, craft supplies, or other small items. The model uses no external OpenSCAD libraries and includes a matching JSON file with seven Customizer presets. Print the body and lid open-side up; tune the thread clearance for your printer.
