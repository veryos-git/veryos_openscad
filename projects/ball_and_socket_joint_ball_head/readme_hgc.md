this openscad is a ball head / ball and socket joint

it consists of three parts: 

    - ball head
        - has 3 screwholes for attachement at bottom
    - socket
        - has 3 screwholes for attachement at bottom
    - screw


the thread 
 (optimized for 3d printing)
is 45 degrees and 2mm pitch (default)



--
parameters: 
- circluar patterend screwholes for attachement : default 3 
- head diameter , default 10 mm 
- baseplate diameter default 20 mm
