TITLE

C-Mount USB Microscope Camera Adapter – 23.2 mm Slip-Fit Tube

DESCRIPTION

Attach a C-mount USB camera to a microscope tube with this compact, hollow adapter. The camera end screws into the camera’s female thread, while the smooth stem slides into the microscope tube. A stop collar limits insertion, and a chamfered tip makes fitting easier.

The adapter has been printed and fitted to the camera shown in the photos. Three thread clearances were tested; the included adapter uses the best-fitting 0.20 mm diameter clearance.

DIMENSIONS AND COMPATIBILITY

- Camera thread: C-mount, 1 inch–32 TPI
- Microscope stem: 23.2 mm outside diameter
- Designed for a tube opening of approximately 23.5 mm
- Insertion length: 20 mm
- Clear internal opening: 19 mm
- Camera thread length: 3.2 mm

Check your microscope’s tube diameter before printing. This is a mechanical adapter without optics; achievable focus depends on your camera and microscope combination.

SUGGESTED PRINT SETTINGS

- Print upright, microscope stem on the bed and camera thread pointing upward.
- No supports required.
- Use 0.08–0.12 mm layers for the fine thread.
- Use at least 4 walls.
- Black filament helps reduce stray light.

Remove any strings or brim before assembly. Screw the camera on gently by hand, then slide the stem into the microscope tube. Use the microscope’s retaining screw if available.

The editable OpenSCAD source allows you to adjust the stem diameter, insertion length, and thread clearance for your setup.
