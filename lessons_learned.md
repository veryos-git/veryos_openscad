## Rules

- Where a screw's head (3) is higher than the room above a part (2.5 under the power-kit turntable's platform), a bracket over the part cannot be flat: its foot lies lower than the part's top. Step it up, foot, riser, arm, and print it on its side: no support, and the layers run along the spring (V6's motor bracket, sliced with the user's settings, supports on, 2026-11-13; not printed).
- A spring arm between a part and a lid over it has a best press: its thickness is the room less the press, so its force goes with thickness³ × press and is highest at a quarter of the room, 0.45 of 1.8 (power-kit turntable V6, from the formula, 2026-11-13).
- Press a part to the side its load pushes it anyway, and put a hard stop there: V6's pad lies on the motor's can 25 degrees from its top, towards the hoop, so it presses the motor down and away from the hoop, where the slot's edge stops the gearbox's boss at 0.1 (2026-11-13; untested).
- In CGAL a skin, one polyhedron over rings of points, renders far faster than a union of hulled segments: finger_claws' claw 39 s as hulled segments (V2), 2.5 s as two skins (V3), the same shape. Do not lay a skin onto a hull of thin discs: the two surfaces are 0.0003 apart, and the STL's six digits turn the slivers between them into edges with four triangles. The union of separate parts costs more than the parts: five sleeves 2.5 s each, 30 s together (OpenSCAD 2021.01, 2026-11-12).
- One script, several sizes at once: set the measures as $ variables in a module and call the rest as its children; the modules and functions under it read them (finger_claws V3's `measures(i)`, five fingers, 2026-11-12).
- 45 degree flanks push a worm and its wheel apart about as hard as the worm drives. Add up what gives way between the two and hold it against the depth the thread has between the teeth: power-kit turntable V3, pin's play 0.15, motor between its walls 0.22, a snap tab that bends until the gearbox's boss meets its slot 0.39, shaft and bore about 0.17, together 0.71 of 0.8 by the model; its teeth skip under a heavy load (print, 2026-11-12). Never put a snap tab on the side a part is pushed to. V4: worm 0.3 larger, the cheapest part to print again; untested.
- First look for the fix in the smallest part: a worm of 1.2 cm³ instead of a base of 45 (the user's idea, power-kit turntable V4, 2026-11-12).
- A platform that stands on a pin at its axis wobbles: the power-kit turntable V1's Ø150 platform, on the Ø6 end of a Ø8 pin 12 long in a tube with 0.3, rocks in the 0.4 gap to the base's wall (print, 2026-11-12). Carry it on the widest circle and let the pin only centre: V3 lies on the wall's top at r 70, the pin 0.6 off the floor, and runs well (print, 2026-11-12). The price is friction × load × radius, 35 times the pin's; graphite on the ridge.
- A ridge one extrusion wide needs thin walls in the slicer. A 0.42 wide last layer in ElegooSlicer 1.5.3.5 (G-code, power-kit turntable V3's base, settings of v2_elegoo.3mf, 2026-11-12): classic walls with `Detect thin wall` off print 69 of 440 mm in 16 pieces, and two overlapping lines in the 0.62 wide layer below; with it on one 0.46 line; Arachne one 0.42 line. `Detect thin wall` leaves the rest of the part where classic puts it, Arachne moves a fifth of the walls' plastic by more than 0.1. Not printed yet.
- A chamfer up to a ridge needs an upright land one layer high at its top, or the last layer is as wide as the chamfer at mid layer: 0.62 instead of 0.42 at 45 degrees and 0.2 layers (power-kit turntable V3, 2026-11-12).
- A part that a screw pulls apart needs its layers along the pull. Softbox V6's light clamp tower, printed standing with the layers across its legs (4.5 thick, 2 walls, 15 % grid), broke off at the roof's inner corners when its 12 mm screw was tightened hard by hand (print, 2026-11-12). softbox_smallrig_40W V1 makes the clamp its own part, printed on its side, with hooks under rails of the frame top; untested.
- A screw that clamps a device belongs well inside its edges: softbox V6's two screws sat 7.5 from the ends of the SmallRig RM 40C, on its rounded back edge, and only one of them locked (print, 2026-11-12). V1: 22 from the ends; untested.
- Before changing a printed design, measure the print file, not the script: the softbox V6 print used 129 × 83 × 36, depth 80 and cutout 15 instead of the script's defaults. With them the unchanged parts of softbox_smallrig_40W V1 match the 3mf's meshes to 0.001 cm³ and stay usable (2026-11-12).
- The 45 degree thread also prints lying: with its axis level with the bed every flank is 45 degrees off the bed; 24 degrees off level, half the flanks at the hole's crown are 21 degrees off it, which works at 5 mm (softbox V6). Bambu Studio adds no support in either (tree(auto), threshold 35). *From the model and the slicer (softbox_smallrig_40W V1, 2026-11-12); the 12 mm thread lying is not printed yet.*
- A pocket in the face that lies on the bed puts a bridge over it. A floor that rises at 45 degrees from the bed lets the wall above lean on the part: softbox_smallrig_40W V1's tripod notch, 49 wide, slices without support that way. *From the slicer (2026-11-12), not yet printed.*
- A finger is not round: seen from the front it is a flat oval, wider than thick (the user's observation, 2026-11-12; the round claw printed before, inside Ø13.4, hurts after a while). finger_claws V2 takes width and thickness at the last joint's crease and at the nail's middle. Its presets, about ¾ as thick as wide, are estimates, not measured fingers. The user's fingers, width only, measured 2026-11-12: thumb 19, index 16.5, middle 17, ring 16, little 15; finger_claws V3 uses them.
- A hard blade in a soft sleeve is weakest where it leaves the sleeve: the tongue carries the whole bending moment there. finger_claws V2, tongue 13.6 wide, 40 to the tip, printed standing: 2 thick about 5 N at the tip, 3 thick about 10 N, at 25 MPa across the layers. *From the model (2026-11-12), not yet confirmed by a print.*
- OpenSCAD 2021.01's preview, not its render, shows wrong shapes when the camera is inside a cutting shape: make cutters a little larger than the part, not 200. Where faces of two differently coloured parts lie in one plane, or a hidden face of one crosses the other, the last part's colour shows on both: keep such faces a layer apart and wrap the parts of a section view in render() (finger_claws V2, 2026-11-12).
- A seat that only cradles a part does not hold it: board and battery fell out of the power-kit turntable's base V1 when it was handled (print, 2026-11-12). Lock every loose part. V2: a bracket over both with a gap of 0.4, seated on its own block, so the screw's force never reaches the board or the LiPo: it keeps them in, but does not press them down enough (print, 2026-11-12). To hold a part down without the screw's force on it, let a spring press: V5's arms are 2 thick and end in pads that reach 0.4 into the two, and lock them (print, 2026-11-13). A snap hook with 0.2 above the part and 0.1 beside it leaves it a bit loose as well: the motor (print, 2026-11-13); V6 adds a second such bracket for it, untested.
- A push switch behind a panel must stand proud by its travel plus the board's play in its seat. The power-kit turntable V1's plunger stood 2.1 proud with 0.6 play and was hard to press all the way (print, 2026-11-12). V2 adds a cap standing 3 off the panel; untested.
- The smallest printed screw is 5 mm (fdm_optimized_screws: 4 bends, 3 broke). Take 5 also where a smaller one would fit by size.
- A DXF from Onshape can hold entities twice; OpenSCAD 2021.01 then imports a wrong shape without a warning: of `veryos_logo.dxf` (8 of 22 entities doubled) only the ring's hole came out. Check the imported size and area, or strip the doubles with `projects/logo_branding_stamp/build.py dxf` (2026-11-12).
- CNC milling at JLCCNC, by its design and threaded hole guidelines read on 2026-11-12: metal walls 0.8, at least 0.5; inner corners R0.5 at a depth up to 3, smaller costs more, sharp needs EDM; a thread at most 3 diameters deep, blind with half a diameter unthreaded at the bottom, modelled as a hole of the tap drill's diameter; ±0.1 without a drawing; upload STEP. *From the pages (logo_branding_stamp V1), not yet confirmed by a quote or an order.*
- A worm on a motor's shaft needs a root wider than the motor: the wheel's teeth come as close to the worm's axis as its root, so a thinner worm lets them hit the motor beside it. With the N20's 10 across the flats the worm's root is Ø11. *From the model (rechargable_motor_power_kit_turntable V1, 2026-11-12), not yet confirmed by a print.*
- Check a gear pair in the model by intersecting it through a turn: both parts advanced in step must never touch, and one shifted by more than half the play must. It catches a wrong hand, twist or phase before printing (rechargable_motor_power_kit_turntable V1, 2026-11-12).
- A printed sliding bearing belongs near the axis: it brakes with friction coefficient × load × contact radius. 1 kg on a Ø12 hub is about 16 mN·m, on a rim at r 31 about 106, three times the 28BYJ-48's 34. A ring further out with a gap of one layer only stops tipping. *From an estimate with a coefficient of 0.35 (28byj_minimal_turntable V2, 2026-11-12), not yet confirmed by a print.* Against it: with a gap of 0.4 the power-kit turntable V1's platform wobbles (print, 2026-11-12), see the first rule.
- A fingerprint in the mesh, sliced and remeshed but not printed (RGBW_pucklight_holder_ringclick V7, 2026-11-12): a logo recessed 0.02 into the face on the bed never reaches the G-code (Bambu Studio 2.9.1, 0.2 first layer); a sealed void 0.4 high, 0.8 above the bed, changes 7 layers inside and no outer wall. Decimating keeps the void and loses the relief; a 0.2 voxel remesh fills the void and leaves a third of the relief.
- A print-in-place slider needs a stop in both directions. The part it pushes is not a stop: ringclick V5's plunger was held upwards only by the puck, and fell out through the top once the screwin was unscrewed to change the batteries (2026-11-12). V6 adds a lip at its bottom.
- On the ringclick click base the holder floats less than the KC007 puck light's CAD model says: RGBW_pucklight_holder_ringclick V2 (`press_height` 1.6, 0.7 travel by the CAD model) did not click reliably; V3 (1.8, click base 8.0 high, 0.9 travel by the model) floats only a little on the base and barely clicks. The holder V3's ribs click with 0.7 by the model, on a solid disc. Ringclick V4 raises only the tube, +0.4, to 7.2 above the holder's bottom; V5's plunger, as high, clicks (2026-11-12). Cause unmeasured: the puck's bottom where the tube pushes, or its lens at the window's edge (2026-11-11, Elegoo Centauri Carbon 2, PLA, 0.2 mm layers).
- The KC007 puck light's lens clicks wherever it is pressed, also at its edge (tested by hand, 2026-11-11): a ring around the window can press it instead of ribs over the centre (RGBW_pucklight_holder_ringclick V1, untested in print).
- Ribs in front of the light show through a transparent lampshade: the RGBW puck light holder V3's 2 × 2 ribs over the dome are stable but visible. V4 tries 1.6 × 1.6 with 0.6 chamfers along the top edges, 29 % of V3's bending stiffness; untested (2026-11-11).
- A rail's end that leaves its slot within the travel gets a lead-in: chamfer the vertical edges of the rail's ends (0.6 on a 2.0 rail) and the slot's ends (0.2). The OMM XY table's rebuild had left Onshape's rail chamfers out up to v3.0.1; the user: they are important (2026-11-10).
- Yaw play of a slider on rails = clearance / guided length along the travel: 0.2 over 61 mm is ±0.19°, ±0.3 mm 90 mm away from the guide. A second rail beside the first does not change it; a longer guide, or a second rail at the far end of the slider, does. *From the geometry (OMM XY table v2.6, 2026-11-10), not yet confirmed by a print.*
- Inside difference() or intersection(), wrap a for() in union(). The online renderer (openscad.ochafik.com) runs with lazy union: a bare for() counts as separate children, so the second screw boss of the esp32_unofficial enclosure was subtracted instead of added. OpenSCAD 2021.01 does not show it (2026-11-10).
- A wall beside a pin header hides what wire sits on what pin. Leave the side open where the wiring has to be checked: the OMM enclosure's ESP32 pocket had full walls up to V6, V7 drops the one toward the drivers (2026-11-10).
- Seat mating parts on each other in the model and check for overlap before printing; the clearance of the main faces does not show it. OMM XY table v2.4 had 0.4 per side in the grooves, yet their square mouths sat 0.85 inside the ring's rounded corners (2026-11-10).
- A dev board bought as a clone does not follow the maker's drawing: the OMM enclosure V2 cradle from Espressif's ESP32-S3-DevKitC-1 DXF (25.4 wide) did not take the 28 wide board. Measure the board, or take the pocket of a printed case that fits it (OMM enclosure V3, 2026-11-09).

- A click base needs no alignment pins: the disc in the screw's opening and the bar in its slot are enough (pucklight_sphere V2, 2026-11-06). Without pins the sockel can turn on the base, so hold it when turning a sphere.
- A screw turned by hand needs height: 8.4 mm high with a 2.6 mm bar gives too little grip and feedback, 10.4 mm with a 4.6 mm bar is better (pucklight_sphere V2 against V1, 2026-11-06).
- A sphere on a neck 77% of its diameter looks cut off (pucklight_sphere V2, starts at latitude -37.8, 2026-11-06).
- Flat faces that set a fit belong on multiples of the layer height, 0.2 mm by default. The slicer rounds the others: a 0.1 mm axial gap sliced as 0.04 mm at 0.28 mm layers, measured in the G-code (pucklight_sphere V1, 2026-11-06).
- A clearance that fits on a small test print may not fit on a large part. Test in the real part, or print the real section (e.g. one corner post) as the test piece. *Hypothesis (babywipesbox, 2026-11-01), not yet confirmed side by side.*
- Glued magnets: check the polarity of each pair before the glue sets.
