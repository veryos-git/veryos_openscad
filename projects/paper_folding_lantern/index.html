Paper lantern

A haunted little house you can hang. Shape the walls and roof, add your own SVG cut-out patterns to any side or roof face, then cut the perforated template and fold it into life.

All dimensions in millimetres. Changes update the real OpenSCAD model automatically.

paperperforated folds

Outside view. The generic window stays on every wall. The pattern is cut out and clipped 2 mm inside the folds.

Patterns are cut through the paper. Use stencil designs; enclosed paper islands fall out. Colors are ignored; convert text and strokes to paths first.
