        Middle/mm

        On the sheet: click a picture to choose it, drag it to move it. Its handles: a corner sizes it, with Shift out of its shape; a side stretches it, with Ctrl skews it; the ring turns it, with Shift in steps of 15°. Ctrl and the wheel size it under the pointer.

        A photo of a drawing

        Taken at a slant? Four points of the picture laid on four places of the cut pattern put it right.

        Matching 4 points

        Over the sheet: what to click next. Zoom in to hit a point. On the cut pattern a click snaps to the nearest corner or middle of the pattern and of its parts; hold Alt to place it freely.
