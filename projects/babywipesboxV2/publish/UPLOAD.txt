Source photo

The closed box with the flip lid open and a wipe pulled through; no
packaging branding is visible. The original photographs are retained in
that folder. The printed box uses the diamond exterior pattern; the model's
default pattern is ribs.

Processing
source/photo_covers.py cuts out the box, lid and wipe with OpenCV GrabCut,
seeded by the dark print and a hand-placed region inside the wipe, and only
white-balances them against the wipe. The backdrop's lighting is divided out
so the studio paper becomes the page colour; the contact shadow under the box
is kept. The print is not retouched or regenerated; only the background and
layout are edited.

Regeneration from the repository root
  python3 projects/babywipesboxV2/publish/source/photo_covers.py

Requirements: Python 3, Pillow, NumPy, OpenCV, PyGObject/Rsvg and Pycairo.
Artwork uses the original veryos_logo.svg and the OpenSCAD icon bundled with
OpenSCAD. Typography is DejaVu Sans Mono.
