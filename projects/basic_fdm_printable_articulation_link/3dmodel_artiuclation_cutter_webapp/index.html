Lay the model flat on the bed

Turn the model so its spine runs along the bed, then cut off the bottom to give it a flat face to print on. The model is centred and placed on the bed on its own; one larger than 333 mm or smaller than 10 mm is scaled to fit 333 mm when loaded.

Draw the spine, head to tail

Click on the model to add points; drag a point to move it; Delete removes the last one. The joints are spread evenly along the curve. At each joint the segment towards the tail keeps the ring link, the segment towards the head the loop.

Cut the joints

Each cutter is sized to the model's width and height at its joint, then all are subtracted from the model. The pieces are coloured; there should be as many as parts.
