Every family has a live, re-implemented greyscale preview: click one to load it into the relief viewer, tweak it, and export a 8- or 16-bit PNG to use as Z-displacement in Blender, a CNC/CAM tool or a lithophane generator.

About the previews. The greyscale thumbnails and the viewer are fresh, simplified re-implementations of each family's general technique (Voronoi borders, erosion noise, domain warping, Truchet tiles…), written for this page — not copies of the linked shaders. For the real thing, open the original and output its height function as fragColor = vec4(vec3(h),1.).
