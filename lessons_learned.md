## Rules

- A seat that only cradles a part does not hold it: board and battery fell out of the power-kit turntable's base V1 when it was handled (print, 2026-11-12). Lock every loose part. V2: a bracket over both with a gap, seated on its own block, so the screw's force never reaches the board or the LiPo; untested.
- A push switch behind a panel must stand proud by its travel plus the board's play in its seat. The power-kit turntable V1's plunger stood 2.1 proud with 0.6 play and was hard to press all the way (print, 2026-11-12). V2 adds a cap standing 3 off the panel; untested.
- The smallest printed screw is 5 mm (fdm_optimized_screws: 4 bends, 3 broke). Take 5 also where a smaller one would fit by size.
- A DXF from Onshape can hold entities twice; OpenSCAD 2021.01 then imports a wrong shape without a warning: of `veryos_logo.dxf` (8 of 22 entities doubled) only the ring's hole came out. Check the imported size and area, or strip the doubles with `projects/logo_branding_stamp/build.py dxf` (2026-11-12).
- CNC milling at JLCCNC, by its design and threaded hole guidelines read on 2026-11-12: metal walls 0.8, at least 0.5; inner corners R0.5 at a depth up to 3, smaller costs more, sharp needs EDM; a thread at most 3 diameters deep, blind with half a diameter unthreaded at the bottom, modelled as a hole of the tap drill's diameter; ±0.1 without a drawing; upload STEP. *From the pages (logo_branding_stamp V1), not yet confirmed by a quote or an order.*
- A worm on a motor's shaft needs a root wider than the motor: the wheel's teeth come as close to the worm's axis as its root, so a thinner worm lets them hit the motor beside it. With the N20's 10 across the flats the worm's root is Ø11. *From the model (rechargable_motor_power_kit_turntable V1, 2026-11-12), not yet confirmed by a print.*
- Check a gear pair in the model by intersecting it through a turn: both parts advanced in step must never touch, and one shifted by more than half the play must. It catches a wrong hand, twist or phase before printing (rechargable_motor_power_kit_turntable V1, 2026-11-12).
- A printed sliding bearing belongs near the axis: it brakes with friction coefficient × load × contact radius. 1 kg on a Ø12 hub is about 16 mN·m, on a rim at r 31 about 106, three times the 28BYJ-48's 34. A ring further out with a gap of one layer only stops tipping. *From an estimate with a coefficient of 0.35 (28byj_minimal_turntable V2, 2026-11-12), not yet confirmed by a print.*
- A fingerprint in the mesh, sliced and remeshed but not printed (RGBW_pucklight_holder_ringclick V7, 2026-11-12): a logo recessed 0.02 into the face on the bed never reaches the G-code (Bambu Studio 2.9.1, 0.2 first layer); a sealed void 0.4 high, 0.8 above the bed, changes 7 layers inside and no outer wall. Decimating keeps the void and loses the relief; a 0.2 voxel remesh fills the void and leaves a third of the relief.
- A print-in-place slider needs a stop in both directions. The part it pushes is not a stop: ringclick V5's plunger was held upwards only by the puck, and fell out through the top once the screwin was unscrewed to change the batteries (2026-11-12). V6 adds a lip at its bottom.
- On the ringclick click base the holder floats less than the KC007 puck light's CAD model says: RGBW_pucklight_holder_ringclick V2 (`press_height` 1.6, 0.7 travel by the CAD model) did not click reliably; V3 (1.8, click base 8.0 high, 0.9 travel by the model) floats only a little on the base and barely clicks. The holder V3's ribs click with 0.7 by the model, on a solid disc. Ringclick V4 raises only the tube, +0.4, to 7.2 above the holder's bottom; V5's plunger, as high, clicks (2026-11-12). Cause unmeasured: the puck's bottom where the tube pushes, or its lens at the window's edge (2026-11-11, Elegoo Centauri Carbon 2, PLA, 0.2 mm layers).
- The KC007 puck light's lens clicks wherever it is pressed, also at its edge (tested by hand, 2026-11-11): a ring around the window can press it instead of ribs over the centre (RGBW_pucklight_holder_ringclick V1, untested in print).
- Ribs in front of the light show through a transparent lampshade: the RGBW puck light holder V3's 2 × 2 ribs over the dome are stable but visible. V4 tries 1.6 × 1.6 with 0.6 chamfers along the top edges, 29 % of V3's bending stiffness; untested (2026-11-11).
- A rail's end that leaves its slot within the travel gets a lead-in: chamfer the vertical edges of the rail's ends (0.6 on a 2.0 rail) and the slot's ends (0.2). The OMM XY table's rebuild had left Onshape's rail chamfers out up to v3.0.1; the user: they are important (2026-11-10).
- Yaw play of a slider on rails = clearance / guided length along the travel: 0.2 over 61 mm is ±0.19°, ±0.3 mm 90 mm away from the guide. A second rail beside the first does not change it; a longer guide, or a second rail at the far end of the slider, does. *From the geometry (OMM XY table v2.6, 2026-11-10), not yet confirmed by a print.*
- Inside difference() or intersection(), wrap a for() in union(). The online renderer (openscad.ochafik.com) runs with lazy union: a bare for() counts as separate children, so the second screw boss of the esp32_unofficial enclosure was subtracted instead of added. OpenSCAD 2021.01 does not show it (2026-11-10).
- A wall beside a pin header hides what wire sits on what pin. Leave the side open where the wiring has to be checked: the OMM enclosure's ESP32 pocket had full walls up to V6, V7 drops the one toward the drivers (2026-11-10).
- Seat mating parts on each other in the model and check for overlap before printing; the clearance of the main faces does not show it. OMM XY table v2.4 had 0.4 per side in the grooves, yet their square mouths sat 0.85 inside the ring's rounded corners (2026-11-10).
- A dev board bought as a clone does not follow the maker's drawing: the OMM enclosure V2 cradle from Espressif's ESP32-S3-DevKitC-1 DXF (25.4 wide) did not take the 28 wide board. Measure the board, or take the pocket of a printed case that fits it (OMM enclosure V3, 2026-11-09).

- A click base needs no alignment pins: the disc in the screw's opening and the bar in its slot are enough (pucklight_sphere V2, 2026-11-06). Without pins the sockel can turn on the base, so hold it when turning a sphere.
- A screw turned by hand needs height: 8.4 mm high with a 2.6 mm bar gives too little grip and feedback, 10.4 mm with a 4.6 mm bar is better (pucklight_sphere V2 against V1, 2026-11-06).
- A sphere on a neck 77% of its diameter looks cut off (pucklight_sphere V2, starts at latitude -37.8, 2026-11-06).
- Flat faces that set a fit belong on multiples of the layer height, 0.2 mm by default. The slicer rounds the others: a 0.1 mm axial gap sliced as 0.04 mm at 0.28 mm layers, measured in the G-code (pucklight_sphere V1, 2026-11-06).
- A clearance that fits on a small test print may not fit on a large part. Test in the real part, or print the real section (e.g. one corner post) as the test piece. *Hypothesis (babywipesbox, 2026-11-01), not yet confirmed side by side.*
- Glued magnets: check the polarity of each pair before the glue sets.
