OPEN MICROSCOPE MOTORIZATION

Three motors move a microscope.

Two stepper motors on a printed XY table move the glass slide; a third moves the microscope's own Z axis. One printed box holds the electronics: an ESP32-S3 and three ULN2003 drivers.

01How it connects

printed, in this project your microscope, your own bracket 4 signal wires per driver 5 V

02XY tablemoves the slide · xy_table/

  1. 1slide holder carries a 76 × 25 mm glass slide, lifts off to change it
  2. 2X slider moves in X on the rail of the Y slider
  3. 3Y slider moves in Y on the two rails of the table
  4. 4table stays put, its window over the light path
  5. 5X motor on the Y slider, drives the X slider
  6. 6Y motor on the table, drives the Y slider

Drive: no gears, no lead screw. A printed sleeve with three O-rings sits on each motor shaft and rolls on a strip of sanding paper on the top of the rail it drives.

03Z axisfocus · your own bracket

The microscope keeps its own Z stage. A third 28BYJ-48 moves it. Its 3D printed bracket is not part of this project: every microscope is different, so design one for yours.

04Enclosurethe electronics · enclosure/

One printed box holds the ESP32-S3 board, the three ULN2003 drivers for X, Y and Z and a 5 V power rail. The motor cables leave at the back, the 5 V cable comes in at the front.

05What you need

PRINTED

  • XY table: 4 stacked parts, 2 shaft sleeves, 4 washers, cutting ruler
  • enclosure: base and lid
  • Z bracket: your own design

ELECTRONICS

  • 1 × ESP32-S3 WROOM-1 board
  • 3 × ULN2003 driver board
  • 3 × 28BYJ-48 stepper motor
  • 5 V supply, rail, jumper wires

SMALL PARTS

  • O-rings, 2 mm cord, 3 per XY motor
  • sanding paper strips
  • M3 screws and nuts
  • a 76 × 25 mm glass slide

CAD renders, a drawn microscope, illustrative colours. Not printed yet: XY table v3.0.4, enclosure V7. Z bracket: not designed.
