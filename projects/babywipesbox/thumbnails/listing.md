# Magnetic Baby Wipes Box — Flip Lid & Removable Cover

## Description

A refillable, 3D-printable baby wipes box with a magnetic removable cover and a hinged flip lid. The capsule-shaped opening gives access to the wipes, while the whole cover lifts off to replace the packet.

The design uses three printed parts: the box, the cover, and the flip lid. Disc magnets hold the cover in place and keep the small lid closed. A short piece of 1.75 mm filament serves as the hinge pin.

Customize the box size, opening, and magnet dimensions in the included OpenSCAD model. The default box measures 200 × 120 × 85 mm before the cover and lid, and uses ten 6 × 2 mm disc magnets. Keep the wipes in their original packet inside the box.
